01 The problem

Skilled deshellers are hard to find.

Dehusked coconuts must have their hard shell removed before they can be processed. That job has always needed skilled workers, and skilled labour is getting harder to find.

The RD500N does the hard part mechanically, so any operator on your floor can keep the line moving.

“This machine can be operated even an unskilled labour.”REX Industries, RD500N product notes
02 Cutter head

High Speed Steel cutter blades.

The rotating cutter head is fitted with blades made from High Speed Steel, the same steel used for machine-shop cutting tools, so the edges hold up through shell after shell.

A brushed stainless guard encloses the head. Only the working edge is exposed at the opening.

HSS bladesSingle cutterEnclosed guard
03 Drive & controls

1 HP, three-phase. Two buttons.

A 1 hp, 3-phase (420 V) motor drives the cutter. The push-button station mounted on top keeps start and stop within the operator’s reach.
